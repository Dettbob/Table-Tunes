Edit categories & playlists

Paste YouTube playlist links, one per line. Several links in one category = one is picked at random each time. Playlists must be public or unlisted; auto “Mix” playlists (list=RD…) don’t work in embeds. For no ads, be signed in to your YouTube Premium account in the same browser (Safari).
